## 1. Clear System Purpose

**GBS School Management System** (Global Brightest Stars Educational Management System) is a multi-tenant, cloud-ready educational management platform built to manage the real day-to-day activities, people, academic records, finances, health care, communications, and operations of educational institutions from enrollment to graduation and alumni life.

### Core Working-System Principle
> *A management system must manage the actual work of the school — not just display pages about that work. Every displayed number, status, form, and button across all 12 dashboards must exchange real information, save truthfully, and give clear, simple, accurate feedback in plain English or the school's chosen system language.*

## 3. Official 100% Student-Based Subscription Policy

*Source of Truth:* [`frontend/src/utils/pricingEngine.js`](file:///d:/version1/GBS_SYSTEM/frontend/src/utils/pricingEngine.js)

- **Pricing Principle:** 100% active-student-based subscription. **Zero fixed tenant fees, zero base package fees.** Inactive, deleted, graduated, or archived students are never billed.
- **4-Month Full Term Free Trial:** Every new institution receives **4 Months (120 Days)** free.
- **Official Rates per Active Student:**
  - **Nursery – JHS:** `GH₵ 3 / month` (`GH₵ 12 / 4-month term`)
  - **Senior High School (SHS):** `GH₵ 5 / month` (`GH₵ 20 / 4-month term`)
  - **Technical / TVET:** `GH₵ 10 / month` (`GH₵ 40 / 4-month semester`)
  - **University / Tertiary:** `GH₵ 10 / month` (`GH₵ 40 / 4-month semester`)
- **Multi-Month Discounts:** `4 Months (1 Term): 0%` • `3 Months: 3%` • `6 Months: 5%` • `9 Months: 7%` • `12 Months: 10%` (with automatic multi-country currency display & GH₵ conversion).

## 5. User-Friendly Plain English & Multilingual Standard

1. **Simple, Everyday Plain English:** Institutional screens, buttons, alerts, and error messages must be clear and easy for students, parents, teachers, secretaries, doctors, nurses, and school leaders to understand — avoiding raw engineering jargon (such as `PostgreSQL`, `RBAC`, `JWT`, `Prisma`, `Enclave`, or cryptic HTTP errors).
2. **Preferred Chosen System Language:** All 11 institutional dashboards automatically adapt to the institution's chosen language across **15 supported languages** (English, French, Spanish, Arabic RTL, Portuguese, German, Swahili, Twi/Akan, Ga, Ewe, Hausa, Yoruba, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese) and **17 country norms** via `TenantLocaleTranslatorController.jsx` and `i18nCountryEngine.js`, while the **Developer Control Center** remains strictly in Standard English (LTR).
3. **Zero HTML Tooltips:** Never use native HTML `title=` attributes or hover tooltip overlays anywhere in `frontend/src`.
